A little about me

My path hasn’t been a straight line.
That’s what makes it interesting.

I started in mechatronics, served as an officer in the Pakistan Army’s Electrical & Mechanical Engineers Corps, and moved into software and project management. Along the way, I’ve led workshop teams, taught at university, and published research in machine learning.

In 2025, I founded Big0, an engineering-led AI and software company. It was acquired in August 2026. I’m now working on agentic project management at Khan AI and studying business at Asia School of Business, in collaboration with MIT Sloan.

This is a place for my work, things I’ve written, and a few moments along the way.
